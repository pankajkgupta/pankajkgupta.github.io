Education

  * Dissertation: *Shaping cortical and behavioral motor dynamics using a real-time closed-loop feedback system*
  * Advisor: Prof. Timothy H. Murphy
* **M.Sc. Interactive Entertainment Technology (CS)**, Trinity College Dublin, Ireland (Aug. 2011 – Jul. 2012)
* **B.E. Computer Engineering**, University of Pune, India (Aug. 2004 – Jul. 2008)

Research and work experience
======
* **Postdoctoral Researcher**, Stanford University, California, USA (Dec. 2025 – present)
  * Studying cortical–spinal cord interactions during motor learning to understand stroke recovery.
* **PhD Researcher**, University of British Columbia, Vancouver, Canada (Sep. 2018 – Jul. 2025)
  * Designed and implemented closed-loop feedback systems to study cortical dynamics in motor learning tasks in mice.
  * Developed computational models (RNNs, HMMs) to analyze neural data and identify learning-related activity patterns.
  * Collaborated on computational modeling of Allen Institute datasets with Dr. Adrienne Fairhall's lab.
* **Research Associate**, Brown University, Providence, RI, USA (Oct. 2015 – Jun. 2018)
  * Used computational analysis of behavior to discover developmental change in memory-guided attention mechanisms in a smart playroom.
* **Research Engineer**, ATR International, Kyoto, Japan (Dec. 2012 – Jul. 2015)
  * Decoded physical actions from near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS) brain signals in an assisted smart house for people with disabilities.
* **Intern (M.Sc. thesis)**, ATR International, Kyoto, Japan (May 2012 – Jul. 2012)
  * Thesis: Estimating a person's object of interest in a smart house using depth sensors.
* **Sr. Software Developer**, Propalms Network Pvt. Ltd., Pune, India (Dec. 2008 – Aug. 2011)
  * Worked on a novel virtual private network (VPN) and virtual desktop infrastructure (VDI).
* **Associate Software Developer**, GlobalLogic, Noida, India (Aug. 2008 – Dec. 2008)
